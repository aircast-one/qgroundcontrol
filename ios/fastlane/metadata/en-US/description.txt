Aircast is a ground control station for uncrewed aircraft, built for flying in the field on a phone: one-handed, in sunlight, with the aircraft in the air.

IN FLIGHT
• Live video with the map beside it, either one full screen with a tap
• Altitude, ground speed, distance to home, battery, satellites and RC signal at a glance
• Take off, land, return, pause, change altitude and speed, each showing why it is unavailable when it is
• An emergency stop that stays pinned on screen
• Obstacle distances and ADS-B traffic when you have a source

PLANNING
• Waypoints, survey grids, corridor and structure scans on a map
• Geofences and rally points
• Upload to the aircraft and download what is already on it

CAMERA
• Photo, video and time-lapse with the storage and mode the camera reports
• Tap or drag the video to track a target when the camera supports it

MORE THAN ONE AIRCRAFT
• Select several and arm, disarm, start or pause them together
• See which ground station is flying an aircraft, and ask for control

CONNECTIONS
• Finds an aircraft on the network by itself; add UDP, TCP or Bluetooth when it cannot
• Optional Aircast Cloud link to fly over 4G/LTE with your Aircast account
• Works with ArduPilot and PX4

Aircast works without an account. Your plans and logs stay on your device.
